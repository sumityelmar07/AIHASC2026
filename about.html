About AIHASC 2027

International Conference on AI for Healthcare, Agriculture and Sustainable Computing · 25–26 March 2027 · Pune, Maharashtra, India · First Edition

1. Introduction & Background

Catalyzing Global Research at the Nexus of AI, Health, and Sustainability

Artificial Intelligence and intelligent computing technologies are transforming healthcare, agriculture, industrial infrastructure, and environmental sustainability. Recent developments in machine learning, deep learning, generative AI, computer vision, the Internet of Things, and privacy-preserving computing offer opportunities to address complex societal and technological challenges.

The International Conference on AI for Healthcare, Agriculture and Sustainable Computing (AIHASC 2027) is proposed as an international academic and professional forum to bring together researchers, academicians, industry experts, innovators, and students working in these areas. The conference will encourage interdisciplinary collaboration, scientific exchange, and the dissemination of high-quality research.

The inaugural edition in Pune, India, establishes a strong academic foundation for future annual editions, providing a transparent, double-blind peer-reviewed setting for original engineering and computational discoveries.

Key Conference Highlights

  • Theme: Advancing Intelligent Technologies for Healthcare, Agriculture and a Sustainable Future
  • Proposed Frequency: Annual recurring international series
  • Review Standard: Double-blind evaluation with minimum 3 independent expert reviewers per paper
  • Scope: 6 Thematic Technical Tracks spanning 54+ specialized sub-disciplines
  • Impact: Addressing critical real-world challenges across healthcare, agriculture, and sustainable systems
💡 Conference Mission: To bridge theoretical computer science breakthroughs with tangible field implementations across medical clinics, agricultural fields, and energy grids.
2. Core Objectives

Seven Strategic Conference Objectives

AIHASC 2027 is structured around seven institutional objectives guiding the organization, peer review, and academic dissemination.

🚀

1. International Research Platform

To provide a premier international platform for presenting recent advances in artificial intelligence and computer engineering across theoretical and experimental fronts.

🧬

2. Health & Agri Innovation

To encourage innovative AI applications in healthcare diagnostics, biomedical research, precision agriculture, and global food security solutions.

🌱

3. Green & Sustainable Computing

To promote research in green computing, energy-efficient algorithms, carbon-aware architectures, sustainable digital infrastructure, and climate technologies.

🤝

4. Multi-Sector Collaboration

To facilitate active collaboration among universities, research institutions, tech industries, healthcare networks, and agricultural technology professionals.

🔒

5. Responsible & Secure AI

To encourage responsible, secure, explainable, and privacy-preserving AI systems that respect user data rights and ethical standards.

🌐

6. Real-World Societal Impact

To support computational research and technological innovation directly addressing acute biomedical, agricultural, and environmental sustainability challenges.

📚

7. Transparent Peer Review & Dissemination

To promote high-quality research dissemination through a transparent, rigorous double-blind peer-review process and suitable scholarly publication arrangements with established proceedings publishers.

3. Participants & Audience

Who Should Participate?

AIHASC 2027 unites a diverse spectrum of global stakeholders:

  • Faculty Members & Academic Researchers: Sharing fundamental research, theoretical formulations, and mentoring scholars.
  • PhD Scholars & Postgraduate Students: Presenting dissertation research, receiving expert peer feedback, and expanding professional networks.
  • AI, CS & Computer Engineering Professionals: Exploring the deployment of state-of-the-art algorithms into production systems.
  • Healthcare & Biomedical Researchers: Collaborating on clinical diagnostic algorithms, biomedical imaging, and medical AI.
  • Agricultural & Sustainability Scientists: Applying computer vision, remote sensing, and robotics to farming challenges.
  • Industry Innovators & Technology Entrepreneurs: Translating research prototypes into viable commercial and social products.
4. Expected Outcomes

Impact & Deliverables

  • Encourage dissemination of high-quality interdisciplinary research.
  • Strengthen academic, healthcare, and industrial collaboration across Western India and globally.
  • Promote practical AI applications addressing acute agricultural and biomedical challenges.
  • Increase awareness and adoption of sustainable, carbon-aware, and responsible computing.
  • Establish a solid academic and governance foundation for a recurring international conference series.
  • Nurture long-term research partnerships, technology transfer, and joint grant applications.
5. Conclusion

Establishing a Foundation for Computational Progress

AIHASC 2027 is proposed as an open, rigorous platform for advancing artificial intelligence, computer engineering, and sustainable technologies through research collaboration and scientific exchange. By bringing together diverse research communities, the conference seeks to encourage practical, responsible, and innovative computational solutions for healthcare, agriculture, and sustainable development.

The inaugural edition in Pune, India, establishes a strong academic foundation for future editions and supports the enduring exchange of ideas among researchers, institutions, and industry professionals.

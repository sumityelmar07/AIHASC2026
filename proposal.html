Section 1

1. Introduction

Artificial Intelligence and intelligent computing technologies are transforming healthcare, agriculture, industrial infrastructure, and environmental sustainability. Recent developments in machine learning, deep learning, generative AI, computer vision, the Internet of Things, and privacy-preserving computing offer opportunities to address complex societal and technological challenges.

The International Conference on AI for Healthcare, Agriculture and Sustainable Computing (AIHASC 2027) is proposed as an international academic and professional forum to bring together researchers, academicians, industry experts, innovators, and students working in these areas. The conference will encourage interdisciplinary collaboration, scientific exchange, and the dissemination of high-quality research.

Section 2

2. Objectives

The conference is organized with the following distinct objectives:

  • To provide an international platform for presenting recent advances in artificial intelligence and computer engineering.
  • To encourage innovative AI applications in healthcare, biomedical research, agriculture, and food security.
  • To promote research in green computing, energy-efficient algorithms, sustainable digital infrastructure, and climate technologies.
  • To facilitate collaboration among universities, research institutions, industries, healthcare organizations, and agricultural technology professionals.
  • To encourage responsible, secure, explainable, and privacy-preserving AI systems.
  • To support research and innovation contributing to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.
  • To promote research dissemination through a transparent peer-review process and suitable scholarly publication arrangements.
Section 3

3. Scope and Thematic Areas

The conference will cover six broad thematic areas:

Track 1: Artificial Intelligence and Intelligent Computing
Track 2: AI for Healthcare and Biomedical Applications
Track 3: AI for Agriculture and Food Security
Track 4: Sustainable and Green Computing
Track 5: Cybersecurity, Privacy, and Responsible Computing
Track 6: Emerging Technologies and Interdisciplinary Applications

The detailed technical scope will include machine learning, deep learning, generative AI, medical image computing, precision agriculture, IoT, federated learning, cybersecurity, edge computing, cloud computing, sustainable AI, robotics, and intelligent infrastructure.

Section 4

4. Relevance to Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

Research presented at AIHASC 2027 explicitly addresses 6 United Nations Sustainable Development Goals:

SDG 2 — Zero Hunger: AI-driven agriculture, crop monitoring, food security, and yield prediction.
SDG 3 — Good Health and Well-being: Intelligent diagnosis, medical imaging, and digital healthcare.
SDG 7 — Affordable and Clean Energy: Energy-efficient computing and renewable energy optimization.
SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure: Intelligent systems, technological innovation, and resilient digital infrastructure.
SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption and Production: Resource-efficient computing and sustainable production systems.
SDG 13 — Climate Action: Climate forecasting, environmental monitoring, and climate-smart agriculture.
Section 5

5. Proposed Conference Format

The two-day conference programme will comprise:

  • Inaugural session and keynote addresses
  • Invited talks by subject experts
  • Technical paper presentation sessions
  • Thematic sessions covering healthcare, agriculture, and sustainable computing
  • Research and industry interaction sessions
  • Best Paper Awards and recognition of outstanding contributions
  • Valedictory session and presentation of conference outcomes

The final programme will depend on speaker availability, accepted submissions, and organizational arrangements.

Section 6

6. Participants and Expected Audience

The conference is structured to engage a comprehensive cross-section of academic and industrial communities:

  • National and international universities
  • Academic and industrial research laboratories
  • Faculty members and research scholars
  • Undergraduate and postgraduate students
  • AI, computer science, and computer engineering professionals
  • Healthcare, biomedical, agricultural, and sustainability researchers
  • Technology companies, startups, and innovation centres
Section 7

7. Peer Review and Publication Ethics

The organizing committee proposes a double-blind peer-review process for submitted full papers. Each manuscript is intended to be assessed by at least three independent reviewers, subject to reviewer availability and the final conference policy.

Review criteria will include originality, technical soundness, methodological rigor, relevance, clarity, and research contribution. Appropriate plagiarism screening, conflict-of-interest management, and publication ethics procedures will be implemented.

Section 8

8. Proposed Publication Plan

The organizers intend to explore publication of selected accepted papers through a suitable established conference proceedings publisher. The final publication route will depend on the publisher's evaluation, acceptance of the proceedings proposal, manuscript quality, and compliance with its requirements.

Indexing Clause:

No guarantee of Scopus indexing is made. The organizers will communicate confirmed publication and indexing information transparently to prospective authors.

Section 9

9. Expected Outcomes

  • Encourage dissemination of high-quality interdisciplinary research.
  • Strengthen academic and industry collaboration.
  • Promote AI applications addressing healthcare and agricultural challenges.
  • Increase awareness of sustainable and responsible computing.
  • Establish a foundation for a recurring international conference series.
  • Encourage future research partnerships and technology innovation.
Section 10

10. Conclusion

AIHASC 2027 is proposed as a platform for advancing artificial intelligence, computer engineering, and sustainable technologies through research collaboration and scientific exchange. By bringing together diverse research communities, the conference seeks to encourage practical, responsible, and innovative computational solutions for healthcare, agriculture, and sustainable development.

The inaugural edition in Pune, India, is intended to establish a strong academic foundation for future editions and support the exchange of ideas among researchers, institutions, and industry professionals.

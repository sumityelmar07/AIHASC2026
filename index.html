Conference Rationale

Transforming Society through Responsible & Energy-Efficient AI

Artificial Intelligence and intelligent computing technologies are transforming healthcare diagnostics, sustainable agriculture, industrial infrastructure, and environmental preservation. Recent developments in deep learning, generative AI, computer vision, the Internet of Things, and privacy-preserving computing offer groundbreaking opportunities to solve urgent societal challenges.

The International Conference on AI for Healthcare, Agriculture and Sustainable Computing (AIHASC 2027) provides a premier forum for presenting novel methodologies, theoretical developments, experimental findings, and practical deployments of intelligent computing.

  • Interdisciplinary Collaboration: Connecting computer scientists, biomedical clinicians, agronomists, and environmental researchers.
  • Rigorous Double-Blind Review: Each manuscript evaluated by at least three independent reviewers for methodological rigor and technical soundness.
  • UN SDGs Alignment: Dedicated research focus addressing Zero Hunger, Good Health, Clean Energy, Industry Innovation, and Climate Action.
  • Academic & Industry Exchange: Best Paper Awards, keynote lectures, and research translation workshops.

Types of Contributions Welcomed

AIHASC 2027 invites multiple original submission formats:

  • Full-Length Research Papers: Presenting novel methods, algorithms, mathematical models, or empirical findings.
  • Application-Oriented Papers: Addressing practical challenges in healthcare delivery, crop management, or energy efficiency.
  • Interdisciplinary Studies: Combining AI, computer engineering, biosciences, and ecological data science.
  • System Design & Evaluation: Practical deployments, hardware/software prototypes, and benchmark evaluations.
⚠️ Originality Notice: All submissions must represent unpublished work and must not be under review elsewhere.

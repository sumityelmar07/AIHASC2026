Invitation to Authors

Submit Your Original Research Contributions to AIHASC 2027

The International Conference on AI for Healthcare, Agriculture and Sustainable Computing (AIHASC 2027) invites researchers, academicians, scientists, industry professionals, research scholars, and students to submit original research contributions addressing recent advancements in Artificial Intelligence (AI), computer engineering, healthcare technologies, agricultural innovation, and sustainable computing.

AIHASC 2027 aims to provide an interdisciplinary platform for presenting innovative methodologies, theoretical developments, experimental findings, and practical applications of intelligent computing. The conference emphasizes the development of reliable, secure, responsible, and energy-efficient AI solutions that address real-world challenges in healthcare, agriculture, and sustainable development.

The conference encourages contributions that advance intelligent healthcare systems, precision agriculture, smart infrastructure, privacy-preserving computing, green technologies, and responsible AI. Research aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), particularly Zero Hunger, Good Health and Well-being, Affordable and Clean Energy, Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure, Responsible Consumption and Production, and Climate Action, is especially welcome.

Submission Categories

Types of Contributions Welcomed

AIHASC 2027 welcomes original, unpublished research across four primary contribution streams.

📄

Full-Length Research Papers

Presenting novel algorithms, mathematical modeling, theoretical proofs, system architectures, or extensive experimental benchmark findings.

⚙️

Application-Oriented Papers

Addressing concrete, practical engineering and scientific challenges in healthcare delivery, field crop management, or energy-grid computing.

🔬

Interdisciplinary Studies

Combining AI, computer engineering, data science, agronomy, molecular medicine, and environmental sustainability sciences.

📊

System Design & Evaluation

Hardware/software prototype implementations, embedded edge testbeds, and thorough performance evaluations supported by reproducible data.

Ethical & Originality Requirement:

All submissions must be original, must not be under consideration for publication elsewhere, and must comply with applicable research ethics and publication policies. Submissions will be screened using professional similarity and anti-plagiarism tools.

Evaluation Model

Submission & Double-Blind Review

Submitted manuscripts will be evaluated through a rigorous peer-review process for originality, technical quality, methodological soundness, relevance, and contribution to the field. The conference plans to use a double-blind review process, subject to the final review policy and implementation arrangements.

Authors should follow the official manuscript template and submission instructions that will be provided on the conference website. Submissions must include complete methodological details, appropriate references, and supporting experimental or analytical evidence where applicable.

Evaluation Criteria:

  • Originality & Novelty: Significance and degree of technical innovation over prior art.
  • Technical & Methodological Soundness: Correctness of proofs, algorithmic complexity, and mathematical foundations.
  • Empirical Rigor: Quality of validation, datasets utilized, statistical significance, and ablation studies.
  • Clarity & Readability: High organizational standard, professional figures, and coherent English presentation.
Official Publication Policy

Publication Plan & Indexing Transparency

Publication arrangements are subject to confirmation with the selected publisher and acceptance of the proceedings proposal. Papers accepted by the conference will be considered for publication according to the final publisher requirements and applicable quality criteria.

🛡️ Indexing Statement

Scopus indexing is not guaranteed. Any indexing statement will be made only after the relevant publication arrangements and coverage have been officially verified by the publisher.

Technical Tracks & Research Scope

Explore the six core technical tracks and 54+ specialized subtopics invited for presentation and peer-reviewed publication at AIHASC 2027.

🔍
Track 01 8 Core Topics

Artificial Intelligence and Intelligent Computing

Focuses on fundamental algorithmic developments, neural architectures, deep learning theory, generative models, and scalable machine intelligence techniques.

Topics of Interest:

Machine Learning & Deep Learning Generative AI & Large Language Models Computer Vision & Image Processing Natural Language Processing & Multimodal AI Explainable, Responsible & Trustworthy AI Reinforcement Learning & Decision Systems Neural Networks & Model Optimization Big Data Analytics & Predictive Modelling
SDG Alignment: SDG 9, SDG 12 Submit to Track 1 →
Track 02 9 Core Topics

AI for Healthcare and Biomedical Applications

Dedicated to clinical computational methods, intelligent diagnostic decision support, biomedical signal informatics, medical image segmentation, and precision healthcare.

Topics of Interest:

Medical Image Analysis, Segmentation & Classification AI-Based Disease Prediction & Diagnosis Biomedical Signal Processing & Patient Monitoring AI for Cancer Detection & Decision Support Digital Health, Telemedicine & Smart Healthcare AI in Drug Discovery & Personalized Medicine AI for Reproductive Medicine & Embryo Selection Federated Learning & Privacy-Preserving Health Healthcare Data Analytics & Clinical Support
SDG Alignment: SDG 3 (Good Health) Submit to Track 2 →
Track 03 9 Core Topics

AI for Agriculture and Food Security

Covers computational agronomy, drone and satellite remote sensing, autonomous agricultural machinery, pest management, and intelligent yield forecasting.

Topics of Interest:

Plant Disease Detection & Classification Precision Agriculture & Smart Farming Crop Yield Prediction & Agricultural Forecasting AI-Based Soil, Water & Nutrient Management Agricultural Robotics & Autonomous Harvesters Remote Sensing, Satellite Imaging & Drones Climate-Smart Agriculture & Pest Management Food Quality Assessment & Supply Chain IoT-Enabled Agricultural Sensor Networks
SDG Alignment: SDG 2 (Zero Hunger) Submit to Track 3 →
Track 04 9 Core Topics

Sustainable and Green Computing

Explores low-power computing paradigms, energy-efficient algorithmic formulations, carbon-aware data centers, renewable grid optimization, and smart urban ecosystems.

Topics of Interest:

Green Computing & Sustainable AI Energy-Efficient Algorithms & AI Models Green Cloud & Data Centre Optimization Edge Computing & Fog Computing Sustainable High-Performance Computing (HPC) Renewable Energy Forecasting & Smart Grid Carbon-Aware Computing & Resource Management Smart Cities & Intelligent Transportation Computing for Climate Change & Eco-Monitoring
SDG Alignment: SDG 7, SDG 11, SDG 13 Submit to Track 4 →
Track 05 8 Core Topics

Cybersecurity, Privacy, and Responsible Computing

Investigates cryptographic protocols, decentralized ledgers, adversarial robustness, privacy-preserving machine learning, and ethical data governance models.

Topics of Interest:

AI-Driven Cybersecurity & Intrusion Detection Blockchain & Distributed Ledger Technologies Federated Learning & Secure Distributed Training Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning (PPML) Secure IoT & Cyber-Physical Systems Adversarial Machine Learning & AI Defense Data Governance, Fairness & Accountability Trustworthy AI for Critical National Assets
SDG Alignment: SDG 9, SDG 16 Submit to Track 5 →
Track 06 9 Core Topics

Emerging Technologies & Interdisciplinary Applications

Encompasses next-generation paradigms, digital twin simulations, autonomous robotics, quantum computing algorithms, and multidisciplinary SDG applications.

Topics of Interest:

Internet of Things (IoT) & Industrial IoT Digital Twins & Intelligent Physical Simulation Robotics & Autonomous Navigation Systems Quantum Computing & Emerging Computational Models Cloud-Native Distributed Architectures AI for Smart Education & Digital Inclusion AI for Disaster Prediction & Early Warnings Intelligent Transportation & Urban Mobility Interdisciplinary AI Applications Supporting SDGs
SDG Alignment: All 6 Conference SDGs Submit to Track 6 →

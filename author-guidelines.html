Author Preparation & Submission Guidelines

Comprehensive instructions for double-blind anonymization, manuscript template formatting, peer review standards, and ethical publishing.

Policy 1

1. Double-Blind Review Anonymization

AIHASC 2027 operates under a double-blind peer-review policy. Authors must ensure their initial submitted manuscript contains no identifying information:

  • Author Names & Affiliations: Omit all author names, institutional affiliations, physical addresses, and email IDs from the title block.
  • Self-Citations: Refer to your prior work in the third person (e.g. use "Previous studies by Smith et al. demonstrated..." rather than "In our previous paper...").
  • Acknowledgments: Do not include acknowledgments, funding grant numbers, or institutional lab names in the initial review copy. These may be reinstated in the camera-ready version.
  • Document Metadata: Clear metadata properties (author name, institution) from the generated PDF before uploading.
Policy 2

2. Manuscript Formatting & Templates

Submissions must strictly conform to professional international conference standards:

  • Language: English (UK or US consistently throughout). Manuscripts must be clear and grammatically sound.
  • Page Length: Recommended 6 to 8 pages including all figures, tables, algorithms, and references.
  • File Format: Initial submission must be in PDF format.
  • Visuals: High-resolution vector or 300+ DPI bitmap diagrams. Ensure graphs have clear legends and legible axes.
  • Methodological Detail: Must include complete mathematical formulation, experimental hyperparameters, and reference datasets.

Section 7 of Proposal

3. Peer Review & Publication Ethics

The organizing committee proposes a double-blind peer-review process for submitted full papers. Each manuscript is intended to be assessed by at least three independent reviewers, subject to reviewer availability and the final conference policy.

Desk Screening

Initial verification of scope alignment, double-blind anonymization compliance, and similarity index screening.

Expert Peer Review

Detailed evaluation by at least three independent domain specialists assessing methodological soundness, originality, and empirical evidence.

TPC Recommendation

Technical Program Committee (TPC) synthesizes reviewer comments and issues formal decision: Accept, Revise, or Reject.

4. Six Core Review Criteria

Reviewers will score submissions across six standardized dimensions:

  • Originality: Novel concepts, algorithms, frameworks, or empirical discoveries.
  • Technical Soundness: Correctness of proofs, algorithmic logic, and technical execution.
  • Methodological Rigor: Robust scientific method, proper baseline comparisons, and statistical significance.
  • Relevance: Direct fit within the six AIHASC 2027 tracks and the theme of sustainable intelligent tech.
  • Clarity: Quality of writing, mathematical notation, logical flow, and figure legibility.
  • Research Contribution: Value and potential impact on academic theory or industrial practice.

5. Plagiarism, COPE Ethics & Retraction

AIHASC 2027 maintains zero tolerance for unethical publication conduct:

  • Plagiarism Screening: All submissions will undergo automated similarity check prior to assignment to reviewers.
  • Zero Dual Submissions: Simultaneous submission to other conferences, symposiums, or journals is strictly forbidden.
  • Fabrication & Falsification: Deliberate manipulation of data or figures results in immediate rejection and notification to institutions.
  • COPE Standards: Follows the Code of Conduct established by the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE).
  • Conflict of Interest: Reviewers with direct academic or commercial ties to authors will recuse themselves.
